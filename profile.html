About

I am an Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity and Coordinator of the Computer Science Department at Manar Al Janoub College in Jazan, Saudi Arabia. I have more than 15 years of academic experience in Canada, Saudi Arabia and Jordan, plus over a decade of hands-on industry experience in IT administration.

My work is where information systems, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence meet. I teach cybersecurity, software security, data security and privacy, IT security systems and audit, software project management, system administration and visual programming. I have also taught MIS, business intelligence, cloud computing (AWS), Linux, networking and programming.

Research. I publish in ISI- and Scopus-indexed journals on AI adoption in higher education and healthcare, lightweight encryption for IoT, agentic AI, big data and blockchain. I review for MDPI, Springer and Emerald journals.

Leadership. I was Head of the MIS Department at Jazan University, and I have worked on program accreditation, curriculum development, academic advising and research coordination.

I'm open to collaborating on applied research in AI, cybersecurity and digital transformation, and to partnerships between academia and industry.
